# CarCare — Cleaner Guide

How to use CarCare on your phone, every day.

---

## Signing in

1. Open CarCare on your phone. Tip: add it to your home screen from the browser menu, so it opens like an app.
2. Enter **your mobile number** and **password**, and tap **Sign in**.
3. Forgot your password? Ask your supervisor. They will contact Vernex support.

You will see **Today's jobs**. The buttons at the bottom of the screen are:
- **Today:** cars to wash today
- **Schedule:** the coming week
- **Customers:** your residents
- **Targets:** your month

---

## Your day — step by step

### 1. See today's cars
- The top shows **"X / Y Completed"**: how many cars are done out of today's total.
- Tap **All, Pending, Completed** or **Vehicle Not Present** to filter the list.
- Search by car number, flat or name.
- Each car shows its **number plate**, **parking slot**, **block and flat**, and any time restriction (for example "Leaves by 7:30 AM").
- Tap the phone button to **call the resident**.

### 2. Open a car
Tap a car to see the details: parking slot, owner, address, the resident's instructions, and any time restriction.

### 3. Car washed? Take the photo and confirm

**Every finished wash needs a photo. Without a photo the wash is not counted.**

1. Tap **Complete Wash & Upload Photo**.
2. Take a clear photo of the clean car (or choose one you just took).
3. **Wait** until you see **"Compressed: … KB"** under the photo. This takes a second or two.
4. Tap **Confirm Wash Completed**.
5. You go back to Today's list. The car shows **Completed**.

**Photo tips:**
- Show the whole car and its number plate if possible.
- Take it in good light, and don't cover it with your hand.
- If the photo is blurry, take it again, then **wait for the new "Compressed: … KB"** before tapping Confirm. If you tap too early, the old photo is saved.
- "This photo was rejected" means take another photo.

### 4. Car not there? Mark Vehicle Not Present
1. Open the car and tap **Vehicle Not Present**.
2. Add a short note for the office if useful (for example "Slot empty at 6:10 AM").
3. Tap to confirm.

This **does not count as a wash**. The office can see it. The resident can ask for this wash on another day; if the office approves, it appears on your list that day, marked **Makeup wash**.

### 5. Once a car is recorded, it is final
After Completed or Vehicle Not Present, the job can't be changed. If you made a mistake, tell your supervisor.

## Other screens

| Screen | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Schedule** | How many cars you have each day for the next week, by block. Swipe or use the arrows. |
| **Customers** | Your residents: name, flat, car and parking slot, with search. |
| **Targets** | This month: washes completed against your goal, day by day, and how many were Vehicle Not Present. |

---

## Good to know

- You only see **your** cars and residents.
- Washes can only be recorded **on the day** they are scheduled.
- Residents see the **date** of their wash and the photo, but **not your name and not the time**.
- Photos are kept for 60 days, then deleted automatically.
- If the app says something went wrong, check your mobile data or Wi-Fi and try again. If it keeps happening, tell your supervisor which car and what you saw (a screenshot helps).
- Always **sign out** if you use someone else's phone.
