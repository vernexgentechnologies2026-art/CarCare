# CarCare — Admin Manual

For the facility's CarCare administrators. Use CarCare admin on a laptop or desktop; it also works on a tablet.

## Signing in

Open the CarCare address in your browser and sign in with your mobile number and password. Admin accounts are created by Vernex support; nobody can make themselves an admin. If you forget your password, contact Vernex support (see the end of this manual).

The menu on the left (a ☰ button on small screens) has these sections: **Dashboard, Customers, Cleaners, Packages, Schedules, Payments, Photos, Activity log.** The bell at the top shows things that need your attention.

---

## 1. Dashboard — the day at a glance

**Operations dashboard** shows:
- total customers;
- **today's washes**: completed, vehicle not present and still pending;
- **completion rate** over the last 7 days;
- **cleaners active**;
- **outstanding invoices** and the amount due;
- a **14-day wash trend** chart.

Pending approvals and open reschedule requests are listed so you can act on them straight away.

## 2. Customers — approvals and resident records

### Approving a new resident
1. Go to **Customers → Pending Approvals**. The tab shows how many are waiting.
2. Open the registration and check the name, flat, car and parking slot.
3. Click **Approve**, choose the cleaner under **Assign cleaner**, and confirm.

The resident can now sign in, choose a package and get washes. New residents can't book anything until you approve them.

### Rejecting a registration
Click **Reject** and give a **reason**. The resident sees your reason on their status screen, so keep it clear and polite, for example "Flat number doesn't match our records — please contact the office."

### Finding a resident
Under **All Residents**, search by name, phone, flat or car registration, and filter by block, status or cleaner. Open a resident to see their car, package, cleaner, service window and recent invoices.

### Pausing or resuming a resident
Open the resident and click **Pause service** for someone who is away for a long period or has stopped service. **A paused resident gets no new washes scheduled.** Click **Resume service** to restart. Both actions are recorded in the activity log.

### Changing a resident's cleaner or package
From the resident's record, assign a different cleaner, or change the package. The new package must suit the car's size. Changes apply from today. Already-recorded washes are never changed.

## 3. Cleaners — routes and workload

**Cleaner allocation** shows:
- **Roster:** each cleaner, their assigned vehicles and today's progress.
- **Block route coverage:** how many active cars each cleaner has in each block.

### Moving a route to another cleaner
Use this for leave, a new hire, or rebalancing.
1. Click **Reassign cleaner**.
2. Choose **From cleaner**, **To cleaner** and the block (or the whole route).
3. Confirm.

The cars move, and any of today's or future washes not yet recorded move with them.

New cleaner accounts are created by Vernex support. Send the cleaner's name, mobile number and blocks.

## 4. Packages — plans and prices

**Packages & pricing** lists every plan with its schedule (Daily; Mon · Wed · Fri; Tue · Fri; Thursday only) and the car sizes it can be bought for.

- **Edit** a package to change its name, monthly price, description, features or whether it's offered.
- **Car size compatibility:** switch sizes on or off for a plan. CarCare refuses to switch off a size that subscribed cars still use, so existing residents are never left on a plan that no longer fits their car.

Price changes apply to invoices issued after the change.

## 5. Schedules — calendar and reschedule requests

### Master calendar
Shows every day of the month with scheduled, completed, vehicle-not-present and rescheduled washes. Filter by block, and use the arrows to change month.

### Reschedule queue
Residents can ask to reschedule a missed wash from the last 30 days to a date within the next 14 days.

1. Open **Reschedule queue**. Pending requests are listed first.
2. **Approve**, or **Reject** with a note. The resident sees your note.

An approved request creates a **makeup wash** on the chosen date for the same cleaner. The 13-wash milestone does **not** go up when you approve. It goes up only when the cleaner actually completes the makeup wash with a photo.

## 6. Payments — invoices and collections

Invoices are issued automatically on the 1st of each month for every active resident, and become **overdue** after their due date. Residents pay online through Razorpay (UPI, card or net banking). CarCare never stores card numbers.

**Payments ledger:**
- Filter by billing month and by **Paid / Outstanding / Overdue**, or search by name, invoice number or car.
- The top of the page shows billed, collected and outstanding amounts and the number of overdue invoices.

### Recording cash or correcting a status
Use **Manual status override**:
- **Paid (e.g. cash received at the office)** records the balance as a cash payment.
- **Outstanding (reverse a payment)** marks the invoice due again, for example after a bank reversal.

A **reason is required**, and every override is recorded in the activity log with your name.

## 7. Photos — after-wash photo audit

**Photo audit** shows every after-wash photo with the wash date, **the time it was recorded and the cleaner who took it**. Filter by date range, block, cleaner or customer.

- Residents see their own photos with the **date only**. Times and cleaner names are visible to admins only.
- Photos are kept for **60 days**, then deleted automatically.
- Photo links expire after 15 minutes. If a photo stops loading, refresh the page.

## 8. Activity log

An **append-only** record of every change: registrations, approvals and rejections, pauses, cleaner and package changes, washes completed, vehicle-not-present, reschedules, payments and overrides. It shows who did it and when. Nobody can edit or delete entries. Filter by action, record type or role, or search.

Use it to answer questions like "who approved this resident?" or "when was this invoice marked paid?".

---

## The rules CarCare enforces

You can't override these from the admin screens.

1. Only washes **completed with an after-wash photo** count toward a resident's 13-wash milestone.
2. **Vehicle not present** is recorded separately, never counts as a wash, and appears in the activity log.
3. A cleaner cannot complete a wash without attaching a photo.
4. Residents see only their own information. Cleaners see only their assigned cars and residents. Admins see everything.
5. Residents see wash **dates only**: never the time or the cleaner's name.
6. Requesting or approving a reschedule never adds to the count; only the completed makeup wash does.
